Privacy & security

Built to stay out of your way — and out of your data.

Voca stores what it needs locally and does not build a transcript history around your work.

  • API keys are encrypted locally using Electron safeStorage.
  • Audio is processed in memory and is not saved by Voca.
  • Transcript content is not persisted.
  • Only aggregate usage and latency metrics are stored locally.
  • Voca has no accounts, analytics backend, or transcript database.

Audio, text, and relevant context are sent to the configured Groq and Gemini APIs when required and are subject to those providers' policies.
